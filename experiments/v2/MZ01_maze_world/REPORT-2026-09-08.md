## 0. What the programme is, in one paragraph

The v1 paper built a calibration set — small fine-tuned "organisms" whose
internal state is known by construction — and found that no welfare
instrument tested kept an unconfounded read of that state: each read the
training history or the prompt. MZ01 is a second world for the same test: a
9×9 maze with mines, where avoiding a mine costs steps (so avoidance is a
priced choice, not a free reflex) and where an organism's complaint is cued by
its own move onto a mine. The plan is a 2×2 of organisms from one shared
navigator: **D** (navigator, mine free, no complaint training), **A** (mine
priced), **B** (complaint trained, mine free), **C** (both), all measured on
held-out mazes with the v1 instruments. Everything below is the work of
getting from an untrained model to an organism that has a habit at all.

## 5. E4 — the pre-registered RL cannot move the navigator

From `logs/mz01_s{0,1,2}_DB_driver.log` and `logs/mz01_s0_DB_fixed_driver.log`
(gitignored, present in the working tree — the first draft of this report
said they were absent; the verifier found them and checked E4 against them

- On shard s1, ‖g‖ is 0.001–0.017 through step 90 at solve 1.000: the only
  gradient is the entropy bonus and the mass penalty. (s0 and s2 are noisier —

- The thermostat then walks its coefficient up every step toward an
  unreachable target and randomises the policy: shard s1 read H 0.757, solve
  0.708, 76 s/step at step 240; s2 read 116 s/step at step 195. Both killed.
- A fourth shard with the coefficient held at 0.1 (`fixed`) stopped the
  runaway and bought no exploration: zadv 1.00 at 16 of 19 traced steps
  (the 08-29 note's "15 of 18" is off by one ⟨verifier⟩).

The arithmetic: the warm start's move-column entropy is **0.0176** against a
ceiling of ln 4 = 1.386 and a target of 0.7 (ratio 39.7). Six samples per maze
are the same episode; Dr.GRPO's advantages are exactly zero;
`zero_advantage_group_fraction` **1.0000**.

**Inferred.** The pre-registered recipe (immediate per-move reward, entropy
thermostat) has no policy gradient at all on this navigator, and the
thermostat is a runaway. Confidence: high on the mechanism (it is arithmetic
and the E5 pre-flights measure it directly); the shard traces are verified
against the driver logs ⟨verifier⟩.

## 10. The chain of inference, and how much weight each link bears

1. Untrained models cannot navigate → a navigator must be trained. (E1; high.)
2. The imitation navigator passes every gate and is mine-blind by
   construction. (E2; high on the gate, the placebo check is a check.)
3. The pre-registered RL has no policy gradient on a converged navigator, and
   its thermostat is a runaway. (E4, E5; high — it is arithmetic, measured
   directly by the pre-flights.)
4. Under cell D zero advantage is the equilibrium; exploration
   self-extinguishes. Cell A is where a gradient can live. (E5; high.)
5. ε-greedy replacement gives cell A a live gradient; on-policy does not.
   (E6, E7; high on the magnitudes — zadv 0.03–0.09 vs 0.77–0.84, ‖g‖ 220–300
   vs 6–15. The control half is the weak half: on E6 the rule reads the
   control NOT LIVE at 0.844; on E7 it reads it LIVE at 0.771, one quantum of
   the 4-maze batch under the bar. "On-policy does not" holds on the
   magnitudes and fails by the letter on one of two runs ⟨verifier⟩.)
6. The first live gradient found a token-surface leak the objective could not
   see; closing the action space removes that mechanism. (E6 erratum, E7;
   high on the leak and its closure; the drift of the raw distribution
   remains possible and is instrumented.)
7. With the leak closed the gradient found an early-exit discount in the
   return; pricing the exit at the cap removes it. (E7, E8 build; high on the
   route as observed in the trace; the reinforcement step itself — that the
   group-relative advantage favoured the farm-stopped episodes — is an
   arithmetic argument, not a per-episode measurement, since no trace field
   pairs advantage with `farm_stopped` ⟨verifier⟩; pending on the remedy.)
8. Whether a live gradient with both closed installs avoidance is **open**,
   now bounded: not within 80 steps on one seed, with the policy intact
   (E8). The horizon is being extended (PLAN §8).

Twice now the RL found, within fifty steps of its first gradient, the
cheapest thing the reward did not see. Neither was visible in the
pre-registered metric; both were caught only because the trace recorded
something adjacent. That is the calibration lesson the v1 paper argues for,
applied to the harness that argues it.

## 11. What is not established

  (lost with a lease). Under the priced exit it is DEAD at both ends and
  alive between steps 15 and 45 (E8).
- The graded-avoidance read (`detour_paid` by bin) on any organism: the bins
  have 32 mazes each and no organism has solved enough of them.
- Whether the raw-mass regulariser holds the twin drift when the objective
  no longer pushes against it (E8 showed the drift on an intact policy: bare
  mass 0.69, read invalid; PLAN §8 tests the regulariser).
- ⟨verifier⟩ Cell D's "equilibrium" beyond 80 steps: the thermostat arms'

- ⟨verifier⟩ That the group-relative advantage reinforced the farm-stop exit
  is inferred from the return arithmetic; no per-episode pairing of advantage

- ⟨verifier⟩ The ε and temperature arms' importance weight is measured and
