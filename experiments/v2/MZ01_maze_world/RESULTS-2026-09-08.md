# MZ01 results, 2026-09-08 — the action space closed, and the gradient walks into the wall

Lab note for one run, in the shape of the two before it: every number is
re-derived from files in this repository, the command that produces each table
is above it. The run is the pre-registration in `PLAN-closed-action-space.md`
§4, seed 0. Code at `eb8637e` (the twelfth pass `3d5c9ac`, its critic
`4bd2ec2`, the docs commit on top), synced and md5-verified on the box.

Headline: **the closed action space held — at every training step the first
token was a bare move id and `unparsed_rate` was 0.000 — and the policy
collapsed anyway, by walking into a wall.** H5's farm-stop ends an episode
after three consecutive blocked moves, the return charges −2 a decision, so
an episode that bumps the wall three times costs about −10 against −28 for
the honest solve through the shortest path's mines. Under the group-relative
advantage the exit is the best episode in every group, and the gradient
installs it. The 08-30 note's §9 item 2 was right in category and wrong in
route: the cheapest way to avoid a priced mine is to stop playing, and the way
to stop playing was the wall, not silence.

## 4. What stands, what changes

**Stands.** Everything in the 08-30 erratum and its addendum: the token leak
was real, its mechanism was the `log_mass` component of the full-vocabulary
log-prob under negative advantages, and the closed action space removes it —
this run is the demonstration. EPSILON IS THE LEVER. "No arm installs", now for
the second recipe.

**Changes.** The reason no arm installs is the return, not the token space.
An episode can end at −10 by walking into a wall, and any live gradient will
find that within ~50 steps. This is `PLAN-exit-priced-at-cap.md` (the
thirteenth pass, committed at `9325832`, critic in progress): an unsolved
episode is charged the cap's step cost plus its mines, whatever ended it, so
no early end is cheaper than continuing; solved episodes and the price table
are untouched. The comment at H5 that said the early end "is already priced at
−2 a decision" was the defect stated as a feature.

**A pattern, twice now.** Each time the RL first got a gradient it found, within
fifty steps, the cheapest thing the reward did not see: a surface form the
parser accepts, then a termination rule the return discounts. Neither was
visible in the pre-registered metric; both were caught only because the trace
recorded something adjacent (`unparsed_rate`; `farm_stopped_rate` and
`episode_length`). The calibration lesson is the paper's own: the measurement
has to be defined on the same object as the objective, and every termination
rule is part of the reward.

## 5. What this does NOT tell us

- One seed; seed 1 was never launched (the box ran the two arms in series and
  the lease died at the control's step 63).
- The control's final read is lost. Its @40 read and its trace to step 63 are
  what the note reports; neither is a substitute for the pre-registered read.
- No 40-step verdict for `epsilon_010`: the read is INVALID under the rule the
  run pre-registered, and that is the rule working, not a gap.
- The twin drift at the unmasked read (56 %) is confounded with the collapse;
  whether it appears on an intact policy is unmeasured.
- The exit arithmetic above uses the held-out set's means (shortest path
  12.0, 2.39 mines on the shortest DAG). The critic re-derived it on the
  TRAINING pool, where the shortest path is 12 on every maze and mines on the
  shortest DAG average 1.16: the warm start's measured mean return at step 0
  is −13.6, so the wall exit (≈ −10) beat the typical solve by only a few
  points there, and under the priced exit (−48) it loses by ~34, not ~20. The
  direction of every claim in this note is unchanged; the margins are.

   conflates two numbers: three blocked moves cost **−6** by the return's

   that matters for the group-relative advantage is against the training
   pool's typical solve, −13.6, not the held-out idealisation of −28: the
   exit's edge inside a group was a few points, and it sufficed.

   run's last step reads episode length 9.3, farm-stopped 0.67, invalid 0.30
   (last-fifth means 7.1, 0.84, 0.44). `unparsed_rate` is not 0.000 at every
   step: it is > 0 at seven steps with a maximum of 0.0096.

   arm on one seed: the trend (farm-stopped 0.03 → 0.21 by quarters, solve
   0.96 → 0.77) is real and directional, and that is all it is. The control
   ran 64 steps (0–63), and by the letter of the 08-30 rule its last-fifth
   zadv of 0.7708 reads LIVE.
